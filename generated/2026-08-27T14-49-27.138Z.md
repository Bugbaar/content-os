AI is transforming software development but it will not replace engineers who understand systems.

The hype around AI often suggests automation will handle coding end to end. However, engineering is more than writing code; it is about designing, integrating, and optimizing complex systems. AI can generate snippets or suggest solutions but lacks the holistic understanding needed to navigate real-world constraints and trade-offs.

Experienced engineers see beyond individual components. They understand system interactions, performance bottlenecks, security implications, and maintainability. These skills require context, judgment, and experience that AI cannot replicate. 

As AI tools become part of our toolkit, engineers must evolve by deepening their system-level expertise, collaborating closely with AI, and focusing on architecture, reliability, and scalability. The future belongs to those who leverage AI to augment their systems thinking rather than replace it.
